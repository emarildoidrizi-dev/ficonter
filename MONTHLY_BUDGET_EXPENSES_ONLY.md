# Monthly Budget — Expenses Only

The Monthly Budget is now an **expense-only** control.

It counts ordinary transactions classified in FICONTER's Expenses bucket.
It intentionally excludes:

- Bills
- Savings transfers
- Goal investments
- Debt / loan / credit-card repayments
- Account transfers and balance adjustments
- Income

Cash Flow remains broader and continues to show all real cash outflows. Only the Monthly Budget / Budget Used calculation is expense-only.
