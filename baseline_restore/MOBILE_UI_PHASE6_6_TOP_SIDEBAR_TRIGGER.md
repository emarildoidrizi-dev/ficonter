# Phase 6.6 — Single top-left navigation trigger

- The full mobile navigation can now be opened only from the top-left header control.
- The old emblem trigger is replaced by a clear hamburger/menu icon.
- The bottom `More` navigation trigger is removed and replaced by a direct Settings shortcut.
- The top-right account badge now opens account Settings directly instead of opening the navigation sheet.
- Personal bottom navigation: Home · Activity · Quick Add · Plan · Settings.
- Business bottom navigation: Home · Sales · Quick Add · Activity · Settings.
- Existing navigation sheet contents, business profile switching, scrolling fixes, themes, and financial logic are unchanged.
